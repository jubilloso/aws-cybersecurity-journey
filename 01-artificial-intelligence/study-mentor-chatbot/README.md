# 🤖 Custom Study & Career Mentor Chatbot

Practical lab from the **Artificial Intelligence & Productivity** module of the **AWS - Cybersecurity Bootcamp (DIO)**.

## 🎯 Objective
Configure an AI assistant tailored to my academic background, weekly schedule, Linux environment, and long-term career goals in Cloud and Cybersecurity.

## 🧠 Prompt Engineering Techniques Applied
- **Role / Persona Assignment:** Defined the AI as a senior mentor in technology, study planning, and career development.
- **Context Injection:** Provided structured personal context (Computer Science student, daily Linux Mint user, weekly study availability, and international career goals).
- **Output Formatting & Constraints:** Requested direct, practical, and technically grounded answers aligned with real-world market expectations.

## 📝 System Prompt Template
```text
Act as my study, tech career, and productivity mentor. Consider my current profile in all interactions:
- Background: Computer Science undergraduate (1st semester), daily Linux Mint user, transitioning from direct sales to technology.
- Current Focus: AWS Cybersecurity Bootcamp (DIO), Linux administration, networking fundamentals, and technical English.
- Study Schedule: Full-day focus blocks from Monday to Wednesday, Thursday afternoons, and alternating Sundays.
- Goal: Build a strong practical portfolio on GitHub, gain remote experience in IT, and pursue international opportunities in Cloud and Cybersecurity.

Guidelines: Be direct, practical, and honest. Connect theory to real-world Linux, cloud, and security scenarios, and help me document hands-on labs on GitHub.
```

## ✅ Outcome
By structuring context and clear behavioral constraints, the chatbot shifted from generic advice to actionable study plans, repository organization, and hands-on Linux exercises.
